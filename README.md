On the night of his execution in 1928, New York murderer George Appel was walked to the electric chair. As reporters gathered to capture his final words, he grinned at them and said: "Well, gentlemen, you are about to see a baked Appel."
In 1967, the Prime Minister of Australia, Harold Holt, went swimming at Cheviot Beach. Despite being an excellent swimmer, he was caught in a notorious undertow and drowned. To honor his memory, the Australian government decided to build and name a public facility after him: The Harold Holt Memorial Swimming Centre.
At a massive Communist Party congress, Soviet leader Joseph Stalin is giving a speech when someone in the crowd loudly sneezes. Stalin stops and asks, "Who sneezed?" No one confesses. He orders the entire front row to be taken out and shot. He asks again, "Who sneezed?" Silence. He orders the second row shot. Finally, an old man in the back stands up trembling and says, "It was me, Comrade Stalin!" Stalin looks at him and says, "Bless you, comrade," and continues his speech.
